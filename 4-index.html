PYXEL ARCADE

Thirteen small 3D games built on the unreleased Pyxel 3.0 development preview (engine pinned at upstream commit c8d2274, loaded on first play; internet required). Arrows / WASD, tap, or on-screen buttons.

ORBIT ORCHARDCollect ten glowing fruit before the light fades. LANTERN RUNSkim a ring of lanterns; dodge the red sentries. MAGNET MAZETurn polarity to pull your way through three gates. SKYLINE STACKCatch moving floors with a single tap. Nine floors wins. METEOR SHEPHERDOrbit the beacon. Deflect falling meteors before its three hearts run out. NEON COASTCoastal-city courier: deliver parcels, dodge traffic, lose your heat. TOWERFALL LABThree stages of real 3D physics. Orbit the camera, topple towers with limited shots. LUCKY CAT COIN TRAILGuide the figurine through three gardens, collecting every koban coin. LUCKY CAT BELL GARDENA three-bell rhythm garden. Tap each lane as a bell reaches its red mark. LUCKY CAT PAW PARRYOne-button timing: raise the paw as coins cross the red window. LUCKY CAT COURTYARD 3DWalk a porcelain cat through a shrine garden. Collect ten koban before time runs out. LUCKY CAT KOBAN TOSS 3DAim the cat's coin toward three lacquer bowls. Nine throws to land all three. LUCKY CAT LANTERN DASH 3DA three-lane run: dodge gates, jump, collect fifteen koban.
